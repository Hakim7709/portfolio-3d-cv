## 🏢 Top 5 High-Volume Fast-Casual & Hospitality Employers in Ireland

### 1. Supermac's (Ireland's #1 Fast Food Franchise)
- **Why it fits:** The direct Irish equivalent of Albaik in terms of volume, fresh chicken operations, fry stations, and high-speed service. They manage 100+ stores across Ireland and actively recruit Shift Managers & Assistant Managers.
- **Careers Portal:** [https://supermacs.ie/careers/](https://supermacs.ie/careers/)
- **Target Role:** Assistant Restaurant Manager / Shift Supervisor
- **Permit Eligibility:** General Employment Permit (Minimum salary threshold: €34,000+).

### 2. Camile Thai Kitchen
- **Why it fits:** Fast-growing tech-forward casual restaurant chain in Dublin and Cork. They prioritize managers with digital automation and HACCP discipline.
- **Careers Portal:** [https://www.camile.ie/careers](https://www.camile.ie/careers)
- **Target Role:** Restaurant Assistant Manager / Operations Supervisor

### 3. Applegreen PLC
- **Why it fits:** Massive Irish forecourt and dining operator running multi-brand food courts (including proprietary restaurants and international QSR brands). Constant demand for food safety and kitchen operations managers.
- **Careers Portal:** [https://careers.applegreenstores.com/](https://careers.applegreenstores.com/)
- **Target Role:** Food Operations Manager / Shift Lead

### 4. Press Up Hospitality Group
- **Why it fits:** Ireland’s largest hospitality operator managing dozens of high-turnover casual dining restaurants, bars, and boutique venues across Dublin, Cork, and Galway.
- **Careers Portal:** [https://pressup.ie/careers/](https://pressup.ie/careers/)
- **Target Role:** Duty Manager / Assistant General Manager

### 5. Musgrave Group (Centra & SuperValu Fresh Food Operations)
- **Why it fits:** Operates high-turnover food preparation departments and retail dining with strict temperature and food safety protocols.
- **Careers Portal:** [https://www.musgravegroup.com/careers/](https://www.musgravegroup.com/careers/)
